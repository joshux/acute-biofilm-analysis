# Implementation recount — 2026-10-28

**Question.** Do the two-axis (growth × matrix) placements survive when the quantification
implementation defects found in the 2026-10-28 referee audit are fixed?

**Answer in one line.** The matrix axis survives untouched; the growth-axis ±300 bp window
defect is real, large, and arm-specific — it deflates the chronic arm most (2.2× inflation),
leaves the acute (1.2×) and COVID (1.1×) FAST placements standing on sentinels — but no
absolute growth number or FAST/SLOW threshold is freezable until the lab anchors are
re-quantified and the full cohort is re-derived with true read–gene overlap.

## 3. The window defect, measured

True-overlap vs legacy on identical alignments:

| Sentinel | Legacy %RP | Overlap %RP | Inflation | Window-only "RP" reads | Class shift |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ERR2275099 (chronic) | 16.72 | **7.62** | 2.17× | 36,447 / 67,679 (54%) | FAST → **ambig** |
| SRR27343249 (acute) | 18.63 | **15.21** | 1.22× | 2,754 / 15,574 (18%) | FAST (stands) |
| SRR26629332 (COVID) | 32.24 | **29.04** | 1.10× | 331 / 3,721 (9%) | FAST (stands) |

Where the spilled reads actually land (chronic sentinel): intergenic/leader UTR 24,728;
**infC** 6,855; rimM 1,137; trmD 711; truB 645; secY 616; rpoA 353; fusA1, tsf, himD, pnp…
— i.e. translation-factor and operon-neighbor genes plus 5′UTRs of the ribosomal-protein
operons, exactly the failure mode predicted. The same neighbor set appears in all three arms.

Distinct RP genes detected: 53 of 53 under **both** classifiers in all sentinels — the defect
does not change which genes are seen, only how many reads are attributed to them.

## 4. What moves and what stands (sentinel-level)

- **Acute FAST placement stands** (15.21% > the FAST threshold 10, even before re-anchoring).
- **COVID FAST placement stands** (29.04%).
- **The chronic arm deflates most.** The high chronic runs (P24M1 19.75/10.06, P77F1 16.72)
  are exactly the ones a 2.2× correction pulls back toward ambiguous — the chronic arm
  *consolidates* toward SLOW/ambig. This is directionally consistent with Kolpen's own
  caveat ("two CF samples had the most ribosomes of all").
- **The FAST/SLOW thresholds (10/5) were calibrated on window-metric lab anchors
  (exp 13.6 / stat 2.1) and are NOT transferable.** The 12 exponential + 11 stationary lab
  runs must be re-quantified with the overlap classifier before any class call is final.
- Projection only (not a result): applying the measured inflation factors to the arm
  medians gives acute ≈ 8.7, chronic-run-level ≈ 2.1 — the contrast direction is preserved
  or amplified, but everything awaits the true re-derivation.

## 7. Remaining work before numbers freeze (the full recount)

1. Re-quantify all arms with the overlap classifier: 16 acute + 38 chronic/lab + 40 COVID
   runs (constant 200k depth, the normalization convention). Sentinel timings (~13–60 s/run)
   make this a few hours of compute plus FASTQ streaming.
2. **Re-derive the FAST/SLOW thresholds from the lab anchors under the overlap classifier**
   (12 exp + 11 stat runs) — without this, no class call is final.
3. Recover or re-specify `norm_map.py` (23 control genes) so the rp/ctrl metric is
   reproducible; recompute rp/ctrl for the COVID growth axis.
4. Fix `quant_final.py`/`quant_any.py` matrix calling to gene-level (≥2/≥3 **distinct
   genes**) and re-run the generalised per-organism and corrected-panel results.
5. Re-run patient-level inference everywhere (chronic: 5 patients; 2BP: best-run convention
   disclosed).
6. Reconcile `COVID-ARM-ASSESSMENT.md`'s "don't use the COVID arm as biology" with
   `COVID-RPCTRL.md`'s defusing of the load confound (already noted, needs one paragraph).

**Bottom line for the paper-type decision:** the implementation recount does not weaken the
two-axis story — the acute and COVID FAST placements and the matrix axis survive on
sentinels, the 2BP burden claim survives correct statistics, and the chronic arm's
inconsistency softens toward Kolpen's own mixed picture. But it confirms that no growth-axis
number in any current table is freezable until steps 1–5 are done. The mSystems M&P vs
Research Article re-weighing should happen **after** the full recount, on corrected numbers.
